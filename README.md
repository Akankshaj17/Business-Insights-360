## 📊 Business Insights 360 – Power BI Dashboard

An end-to-end Power BI analytics solution designed to deliver 360° business insights across Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive functions. 
This interactive dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor performance, analyze trends, and make data-driven decisions efficiently.

---

### 🚀 Key Features

📈 Finance View: Detailed Profit & Loss analysis with Net Sales, Gross Margin %, and Net Profit % tracking

🤝 Sales View: Customer and product performance insights with profitability & growth matrix

📣 Marketing View: Product-level performance analysis based on sales and margins

🚚 Supply Chain View: Forecast accuracy, risk profiling, and error analysis

🧑‍💼 Executive View: High-level KPIs for quick strategic decision-making

---

### 📊 Dashboard Highlights

- KPI Cards: Net Sales, GM %, Net Profit % with benchmark comparison

- Time-Series Analysis: Sales trends over months (vs Benchmark / Target)

- Dynamic Filters: Region, Market, Customer, Segment

- Drill-down Analysis: Region-wise and Segment-wise performance

- P&L Statement: Detailed financial breakdown

---

### 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI (Data Modeling, DAX, Visualization)
- Power Query (Data Transformation)
- Excel/CSV (Data Source)

---

### 💡 Key Insights

- Identified fluctuations in Net Sales trends across months.

- Highlighted loss in profitability despite strong gross margin.

- Region-wise analysis revealed varying performance across APAC markets.

- Certain product segments contributed disproportionately to revenue.

---

### 🎯 Business Impact

- This dashboard helps businesses:

- Track financial performance in real-time.

- Identify high-performing products and regions.

- Improve forecasting and operational efficiency.

- Support strategic, data-driven decision-making.
